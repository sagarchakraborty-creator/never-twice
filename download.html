Never Twice

A habit app with one rule: never skip it twice.

Open in the browser instead

On a computer? Scan this with your phone

Point your phone camera at the code, then tap the link.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap Download the app above, then Install.
  2. If nothing pops up: Chrome ⋮ menu → Install app.
  3. Open Never Twice from your home screen.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open this page in Safari. iPhone can only install from Safari.
  2. Tap Share ⬆ at the bottom.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

It installs straight from this website, with no app store and no sign-up. Your habits stay on your phone. It works offline and updates itself.
